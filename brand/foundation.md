# Numena: Brand Foundation

The strategy core. Everything in this repo — voice, logo, color, type — is downstream of this document. When a decision is not settled here, it is marked open rather than assumed.

## Purpose

Numena helps psychologists reason about their cases with more care, and keeps that reasoning visible to them over time.

Clinical reasoning is mostly invisible. Observations pile up across sessions, themes form half-consciously, hypotheses harden or fade without anyone deciding they should, and decisions rest on a mix of evidence and impression. The reasoning is real, but it lives in the clinician's head and in scattered notes. That makes it hard to check, revisit, or refine.

Numena gives that reasoning a place to live.

## Brand idea

**A thoughtful space for better clinical reasoning.**

"Better" means more examined. It does not mean faster, more automated, or more certain.

**Core claim.** Numena connects what you observe to what you conclude, and shows the path between them.

## Philosophy

Five beliefs ground everything Numena makes. Each names a mechanism, so it can guide decisions later.

1. **Reasoning is a chain, and chains can be inspected.** A case moves from observation to theme to hypothesis to evidence to decision. Each link can be strong or weak. Errors tend to hide in the links nobody looks at, so Numena makes every link visible.
2. **Traceability makes judgment checkable.** A theme that cannot show where it came from cannot be told apart from habit or assumption. When a clinician selects a theme, they should see the source material and the reasoning behind it.
3. **Reflection needs room and structure.** Room lets a clinician pause and revisit. Structure keeps that pause from drifting into rereading everything. Numena offers both.
4. **Hypotheses stay open.** A working hypothesis is useful because it can be revised. Numena treats disconfirming evidence as part of the work, not as a problem.
5. **The clinician decides.** Numena organizes and surfaces. Interpretation, formulation, and every clinical decision stay with the professional.

> **Open:** a second, broader set of seven working principles exists in the brand presentation — complexity deserves careful treatment; observation is not interpretation; understanding develops over time; uncertainty should be acknowledged; trust must be earned through behavior; and related. They refine the five above rather than contradict them, but the two sets have not been reconciled into one. See [Open decisions](#open-decisions).

## Positioning and audience

**Audience.** Psychologists who want to think more carefully about their cases over time. Their pain is not a lack of notes. It is that the reasoning behind a formulation is hard to see, check, and revisit.

**The work it supports.** Gathering information, recognizing patterns, weighing context, exploring possible explanations, and forming judgments without always having complete information. Numena sits at the intersection of psychological practice, information organization, and professional reasoning. It supports the work around psychological understanding; it does not replace the psychologist or automate the complexity of human experience.

**Differentiator: traceability.** This is the claim that sets Numena apart, and it is also the one to verify hardest. We can only promise what the product actually shows: select a theme, see the source material and the reasoning behind it.

| Alternative | What it does well | What it leaves out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Records and notes systems | Store what happened | Show how conclusions were reached |
| General note and knowledge apps | Flexible capture and linking | Clinical structure, and a reason to trust a link |
| Automated summarizers | Speed | Visible reasoning, and clinician authorship of the conclusion |

## Values as tradeoffs

A value only helps when it settles a real decision. Each one below is written as a choice, so the losing side is named.

| Value | We choose | Over | What it settles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discernment | Telling what evidence supports from what it only suggests | Capturing everything with equal weight | Whether observation and interpretation look different on screen |
| Clarity | Plain words and visible structure | Clinical jargon and dense detail | How a screen, label, or sentence gets simplified |
| Rigor | Conclusions that can be traced | Conclusions that sound confident | Whether a claim ships without a visible source |
| Reflection | Room to pause and revisit | Speed and throughput | Whether a feature adds a prompt or leaves space |
| Openness | Hypotheses held loosely, welcome to disconfirming evidence | Settled narratives, one theoretical lens | Whether a feature reinforces or challenges a conclusion |

## The boundary

Numena supports professional judgment. It does not replace it. This is brand, not a disclaimer.

- **The clinician is always the author of every conclusion.** Language, visuals, and features never suggest Numena reached one, assessed risk, or advised on care. The product does not diagnose, provide therapy, recommend treatment, or make risk decisions.
- **Verbs describe what the clinician does:** link, revise, decide.
- **Neither color nor icons deliver a verdict.** No "good", "bad", "at risk", or "confirmed" is ever implied. See [Color](color.md) and [Iconography](iconography.md).
- **No hype and no uncertainty-hidden.** Words like *might*, *possible*, and *we don't know yet* are strengths when they are accurate. See [Voice](voice.md).

## Name and descriptor

**Name:** Numena. Short, easy to say, and not a clinical term. One thing to watch: it may echo *numen* (a presiding spirit) and *noumena* (things as they are in themselves). That can read as mystical, which the personality rules out. The counterweight is plain language everywhere else, so the name stays distinctive without setting the tone.

**Descriptor.** A thoughtful space for better clinical reasoning.

## Brand promise

Complex work, easier to understand and work with — organized, transparent, and open to revision.

- Numena organizes complex information without oversimplifying it.
- It distinguishes what was observed from what was inferred.
- It treats an initial interpretation as a starting point, not a conclusion.
- It makes uncertainty visible where it matters.
- It supports continuity, so earlier reasoning can be revisited.
- It leaves every professional judgment with the psychologist.

> **Open — the promise is tested in practice.** The presentation notes that this promise must be demonstrated by the product itself, and that the positioning is a hypothesis to validate against how psychologists actually work. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) for how strategies get confirmed here.
