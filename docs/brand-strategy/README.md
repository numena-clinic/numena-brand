# Brand Strategy

This directory defines Numena's intended market position, brand meaning, and communication principles.

The documents provide a shared reference for brand decisions and guide how Numena is described, understood, and expressed across product, marketing, and other touchpoints.

## Documents

### [Positioning](positioning.md)

**What place does Numena aim to occupy in the market?**

Defines Numena's intended category, audience, problem, value proposition, and candidate differentiation.

Includes the competitive frame, strategic boundaries, and hypotheses that require validation.

### [Brand Foundation](brand-foundation.md)

**What does Numena stand for?**

Defines the central idea, brand beliefs, name and meaning, promise, guiding principle, personality, and behavioral principles.

Establishes the ideas and values that should remain consistent as the product and brand develop.

### [Messaging and Verbal Identity](messaging.md)

**What does Numena say, and how does it say it?**

Defines the product description, value proposition, core and supporting messages, and elevator pitch.

Establishes the voice, tone, writing principles, vocabulary, and examples used to communicate consistently across contexts.

## How the documents work together

Each document answers a different question:

- **Positioning** establishes the market context and intended differentiation.
- **Brand Foundation** defines the meaning and principles behind the brand.
- **Messaging and Verbal Identity** translates the positioning and foundation into clear, consistent communication.

The documents inform one another but should not duplicate each other's responsibilities. A change in positioning may require changes to messaging, while the brand foundation provides a more enduring reference for both.

## Using the strategy

Use these documents to:

- Guide brand and product communication.
- Develop and review website copy, marketing materials, and product language.
- Evaluate whether messaging reflects Numena's intended position and principles.
- Keep terminology and claims consistent across touchpoints.
- Identify assumptions that need research or product validation.

When making a decision, use the document responsible for that decision rather than introducing another definition elsewhere.

## Evidence and validation

These documents describe Numena's intended direction. They do not establish that its positioning, differentiation, or proposed benefits have been validated.

Keep the distinction between:

- **Established facts:** Supported by reliable evidence.
- **Strategic hypotheses:** Ideas that require research or testing.
- **Product capabilities:** Functionality the product can actually demonstrate.
- **Intended benefits:** Outcomes Numena aims to support but has not yet established.

Use research, competitive analysis, user feedback, and product evaluation to revise assumptions as evidence develops. Public-facing claims should reflect the product's actual capabilities and substantiated benefits.

## Scope

This directory covers brand strategy and communication.

- **Brand strategy:** Market position, brand meaning, messaging, and verbal identity.
- **Visual identity:** See [`../visual-identity/`](../visual-identity/).
- **Product strategy and specifications:** Define product decisions, requirements, and functionality separately.
- **Clinical and technical documentation:** Define professional workflows, clinical considerations, system behavior, and implementation details in their appropriate documentation.

Brand strategy informs these areas but does not replace their requirements or evidence.
