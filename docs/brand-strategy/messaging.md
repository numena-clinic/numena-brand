# 03. Messaging and Verbal Identity

## Purpose

This document defines what Numena communicates and how it expresses itself through language.

**Messaging** establishes what Numena says: what the product is, whom it serves, what it helps with, and why that matters.

**Verbal identity** establishes how Numena says it: its voice, tone, writing principles, and vocabulary.

Together, they translate Numena's positioning and brand foundation into communication that is clear, consistent, and appropriate to its audience.

The goal is not to make every message sound the same. It is to make every message express the same underlying character while adapting to its purpose and context.

Numena should explain its value without exaggerating its capabilities, respect the complexity of psychological practice, and treat psychologists as professionals responsible for their own judgment.

---

# Part I. Messaging

## 1. One-Sentence Description

**Numena is a professional practice platform that helps psychologists organize and examine complex clinical information.**

This is the default functional description of Numena.

It answers three essential questions:

- **What is it?** A professional practice platform.
- **Who is it for?** Psychologists.
- **What does it help with?** Organizing and examining complex clinical information.

Use this description when introducing Numena without assuming that the audience already understands its purpose.

It should take precedence over abstract brand language when clarity is the primary goal.

### Short description

A platform for psychologists to organize and examine clinical information.

### Brand expression

**Better thinking, made visible.**

The brand expression communicates the broader idea behind Numena. It complements the functional description but does not replace it.

---

## 2. Value Proposition

### Think clearly about complex clinical information.

Psychologists work with observations, context, possible explanations, and information that changes over time. Understanding how these elements relate requires more than storing individual records.

Numena is designed to provide a structured workspace for organizing observations, examining patterns and hypotheses, considering evidence, and revisiting earlier interpretations as new information emerges.

Its intended value is to make complex information easier to work with while preserving the psychologist's professional judgment.

**Numena supports the work of clinical thinking. It does not take responsibility for that thinking away from the psychologist.**

The value proposition should communicate a practical benefit rather than depend on AI, technical features, or abstract claims about innovation.

### Value proposition structure

- **Audience:** Psychologists working with complex, evolving clinical information.
- **Need:** A way to organize information and examine relationships without losing relevant context.
- **Product response:** A structured workspace for exploring observations, patterns, hypotheses, evidence, and decisions.
- **Intended value:** A clearer view of the information and reasoning involved in professional practice.
- **Boundary:** The psychologist retains responsibility for interpretation and decisions.

These statements describe Numena's intended value. Their relevance to actual practitioners and the extent to which the product delivers it must be validated through research and product evaluation.

---

## 3. Core Message

### Make complex clinical thinking easier to see.

Psychological practice involves more than documenting what happens. It requires interpreting information, considering alternative explanations, evaluating what supports or contradicts an interpretation, and revising understanding over time.

Numena is designed to make this work more visible and structured.

It helps psychologists organize what they observe, examine how information relates, and return to their reasoning as their understanding develops.

Numena does not prescribe what a psychologist should conclude. It provides a place to work through the information and questions that inform their judgment.

### Core message in one sentence

**Numena helps psychologists organize, examine, and revisit complex clinical information while keeping professional judgment in their hands.**

Use this message as the foundation for website introductions, product explanations, presentations, and other communications that need to explain Numena's purpose.

---

## 4. Supporting Messages

Supporting messages explain the core message from different perspectives. They should be used selectively according to the audience's needs and the communication's purpose, not presented as a mandatory list in every context.

### 4.1 See the relationships between information

Clinical information gains meaning through its context and relationships. Individual observations may become more useful when considered alongside earlier observations, relevant circumstances, and alternative interpretations.

Numena is designed to help psychologists bring related information into a more coherent working view.

**Message:** Bring relevant information together so you can examine how it relates.

### 4.2 Work with complexity without forcing conclusions

Human experience does not always have a single explanation. Some interpretations remain provisional, evidence may conflict, and important questions may remain unanswered.

Numena should provide room for these conditions rather than presenting every question as a problem that can be resolved.

**Message:** Explore possible explanations without treating them as established conclusions.

### 4.3 Distinguish observation from interpretation

What someone observes is not necessarily the same as what that observation means. Confusing the two can make assumptions appear more certain than they are.

Numena's approach should help make distinctions between observations, interpretations, supporting evidence, and unresolved questions visible.

**Message:** Keep what you observe distinct from what you infer.

### 4.4 Revisit understanding as information changes

Professional understanding can develop over time. New observations may strengthen an earlier interpretation, challenge it, or introduce another possibility.

Numena is intended to support the process of returning to earlier information and reconsidering how it fits with what is known now.

**Message:** Revisit earlier interpretations as new information emerges.

### 4.5 Keep professional judgment with the psychologist

Technology can help organize information and support examination of possible relationships. It should not be presented as the final authority on what a person means, needs, or experiences.

Numena's communication should reinforce the psychologist's responsibility and autonomy.

**Message:** Use the structure to support your reasoning, not replace it.

### 4.6 Let technology serve the work

AI may be useful for specific tasks within Numena, but AI itself is not the central value proposition.

Communicate a capability in terms of what it helps the psychologist do, what its limitations are, and how its output should be interpreted.

**Message:** Technology should make the work easier to examine, not make decisions on your behalf.

This message should only be used to describe capabilities that the product actually supports.

## 5. Elevator Pitch

### Short version

Numena is a professional practice platform for psychologists. It is designed to help them organize complex clinical information, examine patterns and hypotheses, and revisit their understanding over time, while keeping professional judgment in their hands.

### Conversational version

Psychologists work with complex information that develops over time. Observations, context, possible explanations, and earlier decisions can be difficult to consider together when they are spread across different records and tools.

Numena is being designed around that part of the work. It provides a structured place to organize information, examine relationships, consider alternative explanations, and revisit earlier thinking as new information emerges.

The idea is to make the work easier to examine without pretending that the product can determine what the information ultimately means.

### Extended version

Numena is a professional practice platform for psychologists who need to work with complex, evolving clinical information.

Rather than focusing only on recording and retrieving information, Numena aims to support the work involved in examining it: connecting observations, exploring patterns and hypotheses, considering evidence, and revisiting interpretations over time.

Its purpose is to give psychologists a clearer way to work with the information behind their professional decisions, without presenting technology as a substitute for their judgment.

Use the short version for introductions, the conversational version for discussions and demonstrations, and the extended version when a fuller explanation is needed.

---

## 6. Messaging Hierarchy

When space is limited, communicate Numena in the following order:

1. **What it is:** A professional practice platform for psychologists.
2. **What it helps with:** Organizing and examining complex clinical information.
3. **Why it matters:** Related information and evolving interpretations can be easier to examine when they are structured and connected.
4. **What principle guides it:** Professional judgment remains with the psychologist.
5. **What broader idea it expresses:** Better thinking, made visible.

The hierarchy prioritizes product comprehension before conceptual brand expression.

Not every communication needs all five levels. A product interface may need only a clear instruction. A homepage may need the description, value proposition, and supporting evidence. A brand presentation may give greater attention to the broader idea.

# Part II. Verbal Identity

## 7. Voice

### Thoughtful, clear, calm, respectful, and discerning.

Numena's voice is its consistent character in language. It should remain recognizable across the website, product interface, onboarding, notifications, educational material, and marketing.

### Thoughtful

Numena chooses words carefully, acknowledges relevant nuance, and avoids jumping to conclusions.

- Do: distinguish observations from interpretations.
- Do: acknowledge competing possibilities when they matter.
- Avoid: presenting a quick interpretation as a complete explanation.

### Clear

Numena communicates in plain, precise language. It favors concrete explanations over unnecessary abstraction or jargon.

- Do: explain what a feature does and why it matters.
- Do: use familiar language when it communicates the idea accurately.
- Avoid: making simple ideas sound sophisticated for the sake of authority.

### Calm

Numena communicates without manufactured urgency, dramatic claims, or exaggerated enthusiasm.

- Do: state relevant information directly.
- Do: use measured language when discussing sensitive topics.
- Avoid: fear-based persuasion, hype, and unnecessary exclamation marks.

### Respectful

Numena treats psychologists as capable professionals. It supports their work without lecturing, patronizing, flattering, or presuming familiarity.

- Do: offer useful information and let the reader evaluate it.
- Do: acknowledge professional responsibility.
- Avoid: telling practitioners what they should already know or implying that the product knows better than they do.

### Discerning

Numena distinguishes useful information from noise and confidence from certainty. It values substance over novelty and restraint over performance.

- Do: make claims proportionate to the available evidence.
- Do: explain limitations when they materially affect interpretation.
- Avoid: technological hype, unsupported authority, and claims that turn possibilities into facts.

These qualities should be demonstrated in the writing itself rather than repeatedly announced to the audience.

---

## 8. Tone

The voice remains consistent. Tone changes according to the situation, the audience's needs, and the consequences of the communication.

| Context | Tone | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Product explanation | Direct and informative | Explain what the product does and what the user can do with it. |
| Instructions | Clear and practical | Give actionable steps without unnecessary commentary. |
| Complex concepts | Reflective and precise | Preserve important nuance without making the explanation difficult to follow. |
| Sensitive topics | Considerate and measured | Avoid judgment, sensationalism, and language that reduces people to labels. |
| Uncertainty or limitations | Honest and explicit | State what is known, what is inferred, and what remains uncertain when relevant. |
| Errors and problems | Calm and constructive | Explain what happened and what the user can do next, without blame. |
| Marketing | Specific and restrained | Explain the intended value without unsupported promises or manufactured urgency. |
| Onboarding | Welcoming and useful | Help users understand the next step without excessive praise or forced friendliness. |
| AI-generated suggestions | Transparent and provisional | Make the status of suggestions clear and leave room for review and disagreement. |

Tone should also reflect the stakes. A minor interface instruction can be brief. A message about uncertain or potentially consequential information may need more context and explicit limitations.

Calm does not mean vague. Respectful does not mean distant. Careful does not mean hesitant.

## 9. Writing Principles

### 9.1 Lead with meaning

Start with the information the reader needs. Avoid long introductions, vague openings, and statements that sound important without communicating anything specific.

### 9.2 Prefer plain language

Use familiar words whenever they express the meaning accurately. Use technical or clinical terminology when it adds precision or is expected by the intended audience.

Plain language is not the same as oversimplification.

### 9.3 Be precise without being exhaustive

Include the detail needed to understand the message and make an informed decision. Do not list every qualification when it does not change the meaning.

### 9.4 Respect complexity

Simplify the expression, not the underlying reality. Avoid presenting complex human experiences as though they have one obvious cause or universally correct interpretation.

### 9.5 Distinguish facts, interpretations, and possibilities

Do not make an inference sound like an observation or a possibility sound like an established fact. Make these distinctions explicit when they affect how information should be understood.

### 9.6 Explain rather than persuade

Give readers meaningful reasons to understand a claim. Avoid pressure, manufactured urgency, emotional manipulation, and unsupported comparisons.

### 9.7 Prefer substance over slogans

Conceptual language can express Numena's identity, but it should be supported by concrete meaning. Explain the product before relying on a memorable phrase.

### 9.8 Be concise, not abrupt

Remove unnecessary words while preserving context and consideration. Brevity should make communication easier, not colder or less useful.

### 9.9 Use confidence carefully

State established information directly. Acknowledge uncertainty where it matters. Avoid both unjustified certainty and excessive hedging.

### 9.10 Respect the reader's autonomy

Provide information that helps psychologists examine a question or make a decision. Do not use language that implies the product has the final answer or that the reader should accept its output without examination.

### 9.11 Avoid artificial personhood

Numena can communicate naturally without pretending to have human feelings, personal experiences, or an intimate relationship with the user.

### 9.12 Make every claim accountable

Product capabilities, benefits, comparisons, and outcome claims should reflect what can actually be demonstrated. Aspirations should be described as intentions, not as proven results.

## 10. Preferred Words and Expressions

Numena should favor words that describe concrete actions, careful examination, and the development of understanding.

| Prefer | Why it fits |
|---|---|
| Understand | Describes a goal without promising certainty. |
| Observe | Separates what is noticed from what it might mean. |
| Examine | Communicates careful consideration. |
| Explore | Leaves room for possibilities. |
| Consider | Supports reflection without prescribing a conclusion. |
| Organize | Describes a concrete action. |
| Connect | Communicates relationships between information. |
| Context | Acknowledges the circumstances that shape meaning. |
| Evidence | Emphasizes the support for a claim or interpretation. |
| Hypothesis | Makes a provisional explanation explicit. |
| Interpretation | Distinguishes meaning-making from direct observation. |
| Pattern | Describes a possible relationship without necessarily asserting causation. |
| Uncertainty | Names a real limitation or open question. |
| Revisit | Communicates that understanding can change. |
| Reflect | Allows for deliberate reconsideration. |
| Clarify | Describes making something easier to understand. |
| Develop | Allows understanding to evolve. |
| Judgment | Recognizes the role of professional reasoning. |
| Possible | Communicates that a claim is not established. |
| Revise | Makes change and correction acceptable. |

These are preferred vocabulary choices, not mandatory words. Use them when they fit the meaning. Do not insert them simply to make the writing sound like Numena.

### Expressions that fit Numena

- Make thinking visible.
- Examine what the information might mean.
- Consider alternative explanations.
- Distinguish what is observed from what is inferred.
- Keep relevant context in view.
- Revisit your understanding as new information emerges.
- Explore relationships between observations.
- Make complex information easier to work with.
- Keep interpretations open to revision.

## 11. Words and Expressions to Avoid

Avoid language that introduces unsupported certainty, technological hype, unnecessary abstraction, or inappropriate authority.

| Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|
| Revolutionary | Inflated and difficult to substantiate. |
| Game-changing | Promotional rather than informative. |
| Unlock your potential | Generic and vague. |
| Transform your practice | Implies broad benefits without specifying them. |
| AI-powered intelligence | Centers technology without explaining practical value. |
| Your AI clinical expert | Implies clinical authority that the product should not claim. |
| The right answer | Suggests certainty where interpretation may remain open. |
| Discover the truth | Overstates what a tool can establish. |
| Eliminate uncertainty | Makes an unrealistic promise. |
| Diagnose with confidence | Risks overstating clinical capability. |
| Effortless understanding | Minimizes the difficulty of complex work. |
| Seamless experience | Generic unless a specific experience is being described. |
| Cutting-edge | Emphasizes novelty over usefulness. |
| Optimize everything | Vague and excessively mechanistic. |
| Never miss an insight | Makes an absolute promise that is difficult to support. |
| Your intelligent companion | Anthropomorphic and vague unless a specific, justified product role is meant. |
| Guaranteed clarity | Promises an outcome that cannot be assured. |
| Objective interpretation | Can falsely imply that an interpretation is free from assumptions or context. |

This is not a mechanical blacklist. Context matters. The rule is to avoid language that misrepresents Numena's capabilities, role, or evidence.

### Avoid unhelpful writing patterns

- Artificial urgency and countdown-style pressure.
- Excessive exclamation marks.
- Overuse of rhetorical questions.
- Unexplained acronyms and technical jargon.
- Long strings of abstract nouns.
- Absolute claims such as *always*, *never*, and *guaranteed* when they are not justified.
- Repetitive references to AI as the source of value.
- Sentimental or overly familiar language.
- Claims of superiority without comparative evidence.
- Reassuring language that hides a genuine limitation.
- Language that treats an AI-generated suggestion as a clinical fact.

## 12. Verbal Identity in Practice

The following examples show how the principles translate into actual writing.

### Product description

**Instead of:**

"Unlock powerful AI insights to transform your clinical practice."

**Write:**

"Organize clinical information and examine possible relationships between observations."

Why: The revised version describes a practical activity without promising transformation or treating AI output as inherently insightful.

### Interpretation

**Instead of:**

"Numena knows what your client's behavior means."

**Write:**

"Examine observations and consider possible interpretations."

Why: The revised version preserves the distinction between information and the meaning assigned to it.

### Information management

**Instead of:**

"Never miss an important insight again."

**Write:**

"Keep related observations organized so you can return to them later."

Why: The revised version describes a concrete use without promising that nothing important will be missed.

### Product value

**Instead of:**

"Get the answers you need, instantly."

**Write:**

"Bring relevant information together to examine it more clearly."

Why: The revised version describes the intended benefit without implying that the product can produce definitive answers.

### AI capability

**Instead of:**

"Let AI tell you what your clinical data reveals."

**Write:**

"Explore AI-generated suggestions, review their supporting information, and decide whether they are relevant to your work."

Why: The revised version makes the role of AI explicit while preserving the psychologist's responsibility to evaluate its output. Use this wording only if the actual product supports those actions.

### Sensitive communication

**Instead of:**

"Your client is clearly showing signs of..."

**Write:**

"These observations may be relevant to your current hypothesis. Consider the context and any information that supports or contradicts it."

Why: The revised version avoids turning limited observations into a definitive conclusion.

### Promotional copy

**Instead of:**

"The future of psychology is here."

**Write:**

"Numena is being developed to help psychologists organize and examine complex clinical information."

Why: The revised version explains the product rather than relying on novelty or an unsupported claim about the future.

## 13. Applying Verbal Identity Across Touchpoints

The principles remain consistent, but their expression should reflect the communication's purpose.

### Website

Lead with what Numena is and whom it serves. Explain the problem, the intended value, and how the product works before introducing broader brand ideas.

Avoid making visitors infer the product's purpose from a tagline alone.

### Product interface

Prioritize clarity, brevity, and action. Use labels and instructions that describe what users can do or what will happen next.

Avoid turning ordinary interface elements into marketing messages.

### Onboarding

Help users understand the product's structure and how to begin. Explain unfamiliar concepts when needed, without assuming that psychologists lack relevant professional knowledge.

### Notifications and errors

State what happened, what requires attention, and what the user can do next. Do not blame the user, manufacture urgency, or hide important limitations behind reassuring language.

### AI-generated content

Clearly distinguish generated suggestions from recorded observations, user-authored interpretations, and established information. Where relevant, make uncertainty and supporting context visible.

The wording should reflect the actual capabilities and limitations of the system, not imply safeguards that have not been implemented.

### Marketing and educational content

Explain ideas with enough context to make them useful. Support factual and comparative claims with appropriate evidence. Do not use educational content merely to manufacture authority or imply that Numena has validated capabilities it does not yet demonstrate.

## 14. The Verbal Identity Test

Before publishing copy, ask:

1. **Is it clear?** Can the intended reader understand the point without unnecessary effort?
2. **Is it concrete?** Does it explain what Numena does, offers, or means?
3. **Is it precise?** Does the language preserve important distinctions?
4. **Is it honest?** Does the claim match the available evidence and actual product capabilities?
5. **Is it respectful?** Does it treat psychologists as capable professionals?
6. **Is it appropriately restrained?** Does it avoid unnecessary hype, pressure, and complexity?
7. **Does it fit the context?** Is the tone appropriate to the situation and its stakes?
8. **Does it preserve professional judgment?** Does it avoid presenting the product or its outputs as the final authority?

Not every message needs to demonstrate every quality equally. A short button label, an onboarding explanation, and a detailed product page have different requirements. They should all, however, remain consistent with Numena's character and responsibilities.

## Validation Note

This document establishes Numena's intended messaging and verbal identity. It does not establish that the proposed value proposition is already validated by the market.

The one-sentence description should be tested for comprehension. The value proposition and supporting messages should be tested with practicing psychologists to determine whether they describe meaningful needs in language that feels relevant to their work.

Competitive claims should be checked against actual alternatives. Product claims should be checked against implemented and evaluated capabilities.

Revise the messaging when research reveals that practitioners understand the product differently, prioritize different needs, or find the language unclear or unconvincing.
