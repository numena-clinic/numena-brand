# Verbal Identity

## Purpose

This document defines how **Numena speaks and expresses itself through language**.

It establishes the voice, tone, and writing principles that should remain consistent across the website, product interface, onboarding, notifications, educational content, and other communications.

Verbal identity translates Numena's personality into language.

It should make Numena recognizable not through catchphrases or a fixed writing style, but through consistent choices in how it communicates.

---

## 1. Voice

### Thoughtful, clear, calm, respectful, and discerning.

Numena's voice is consistent across contexts. Its tone may adapt to the situation, but its underlying character remains the same.

**Thoughtful**

Numena chooses its words carefully. It acknowledges nuance when it matters and avoids jumping to conclusions.

**Clear**

Numena expresses ideas in plain, precise language. It favors concrete explanations over jargon, abstraction, or unnecessary detail.

**Calm**

Numena communicates without drama, urgency, or exaggerated enthusiasm. It does not compete for attention.

**Respectful**

Numena treats psychologists as capable professionals. It does not lecture, patronize, flatter, or presume familiarity.

**Discerning**

Numena distinguishes meaningful information from noise and confidence from certainty. It favors substance over novelty and restraint over performance.

These qualities should be evident in the language itself, not merely stated in brand communications.

---

## 2. Tone

The voice stays consistent. The tone adapts to the context.

### Informational

**Be direct and precise.**

Explain what something is, how it works, or why it matters. Lead with the relevant information rather than an elaborate introduction.

### Reflective

**Allow room for thought.**

When discussing complex ideas, acknowledge relevant nuance and competing possibilities. Avoid forcing a neat conclusion when the subject does not support one.

### Instructional

**Guide without lecturing.**

Give clear, actionable instructions. Explain unfamiliar concepts when useful, without assuming the reader needs basic concepts explained unnecessarily.

### Sensitive

**Be considerate without becoming sentimental.**

Use measured, nonjudgmental language around psychological topics. Avoid dramatic framing, emotional manipulation, or language that reduces people to labels.

### Uncertain

**Be honest about what is known.**

State limitations and uncertainty plainly. Distinguish facts, interpretations, possibilities, and unanswered questions when the distinction matters.

### Promotional

**Explain the value without exaggerating it.**

Describe what Numena does and why it may be useful. Avoid grand promises, inflated claims, and technological hype.

---

## 3. Writing Principles

### Lead with meaning

Start with the information the reader needs. Avoid lengthy preambles, vague openings, and statements that sound important without saying anything specific.

### Prefer plain language

Use familiar words whenever they communicate the idea accurately. Technical terminology is appropriate when it adds precision or serves the audience.

### Be precise without being exhaustive

Include the detail needed to understand the point. Do not explain every qualification or possibility when it does not materially change the meaning.

### Respect complexity

Simplify the expression, not the underlying truth. Avoid turning complex human experiences into definitive explanations or universal rules.

### Avoid false certainty

Do not make a claim sound more certain than its evidence warrants. Use qualifiers when they communicate a real limitation, not as a substitute for clear writing.

### Explain rather than persuade

Give readers meaningful reasons to understand or trust a claim. Avoid pressure, manufactured urgency, exaggerated benefits, and unsupported comparisons.

### Prefer substance over slogans

Conceptual language can express Numena's brand, but it should be supported by concrete meaning. Do not rely on abstract phrases when a direct explanation would be more useful.

### Be concise, not abrupt

Remove unnecessary words while preserving context, clarity, and consideration. Brevity should make communication easier, not colder.

### Avoid unnecessary anthropomorphism

Numena can communicate naturally without pretending to have human feelings, personal experiences, or a relationship it cannot genuinely sustain.

### Use confidence carefully

State established information directly. Acknowledge uncertainty where appropriate. Neither excessive hedging nor absolute language should distort the message.

## 4. Preferred Words and Expressions

Numena should favor language that describes observable actions, meaningful distinctions, and practical value.

| Prefer | Why |
|---|---|
| Understand | Describes a meaningful goal without promising certainty |
| Explore | Leaves room for possibilities |
| Examine | Suggests careful consideration |
| Observe | Separates what is noticed from what it might mean |
| Consider | Supports reflection without prescribing a conclusion |
| Evidence | Grounds claims in support |
| Patterns | Describes relationships without necessarily asserting causation |
| Hypotheses | Makes provisional interpretations explicit |
| Context | Acknowledges that information depends on circumstances |
| Uncertainty | Names a real limitation |
| Reflect | Allows for deliberate reconsideration |
| Revisit | Communicates that understanding can change |
| Clarify | Expresses improvement without overpromising |
| Organize | Describes a concrete, understandable action |
| Develop | Allows understanding to evolve |
| Judgment | Recognizes the role of professional reasoning |

These are preferred words, not mandatory vocabulary. Use them when they fit the meaning.

### Expressions that fit Numena

- Make thinking visible.
- Explore what the information might mean.
- Consider alternative explanations.
- Distinguish what is known from what is inferred.
- Revisit your understanding as new information emerges.
- Make complex information easier to navigate.
- Keep the context in view.

---

## 5. Words and Expressions to Avoid

Avoid language that introduces unsupported certainty, technological hype, unnecessary abstraction, or inappropriate authority.

| Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|
| Revolutionary | Inflated and difficult to substantiate |
| Game-changing | Promotional rather than informative |
| Unlock your potential | Generic and vague |
| Transform your practice | Implies broad benefits without specifying them |
| AI-powered intelligence | Centers technology without explaining the value |
| Your AI clinical expert | Implies clinical authority |
| The right answer | Suggests certainty where interpretation may remain open |
| Discover the truth | Overstates what a tool can establish |
| Eliminate uncertainty | Makes an unrealistic promise |
| Diagnose with confidence | Risks overstating clinical capability |
| Effortless understanding | Minimizes the difficulty of complex work |
| Seamless experience | Generic unless a specific experience is being described |
| Cutting-edge | Emphasizes novelty over usefulness |
| Optimize everything | Vague and excessively mechanistic |

This list is not a blanket prohibition on individual words. Context matters. The rule is to avoid unsupported claims and language that misrepresents Numena's role.

### Avoid unnecessary language patterns

- Excessive exclamation marks.
- Artificial urgency and countdown-style pressure.
- Overuse of rhetorical questions.
- Unexplained acronyms and technical jargon.
- Long strings of abstract nouns.
- Absolute claims such as *always*, *never*, or *guaranteed* when they are not justified.
- Repetitive references to AI as the source of value.
- Sentimental or overly familiar language that does not suit the context.

## 6. Verbal Identity in Practice

The following examples illustrate how Numena's principles affect actual writing.

**Instead of:**

"Unlock powerful AI insights to transform your clinical practice."

**Write:**

"Explore patterns and possible interpretations in your clinical information."

**Instead of:**

"Numena knows what your clients' behavior means."

**Write:**

"Examine observations and consider what they might mean."

**Instead of:**

"Never miss an important insight again."

**Write:**

"Keep observations and related notes organized so you can revisit them."

**Instead of:**

"Get the answers you need, instantly."

**Write:**

"Bring relevant information together to examine it more clearly."

**Instead of:**

"Your intelligent clinical companion is here to help."

**Write:**

"Numena provides a structured space for working with clinical information."

The preferred versions are not simply quieter. They are more specific about what the product does and more careful about what it claims.

---

## 7. The Verbal Identity Test

Before publishing copy, ask:

1. **Is it clear?** Can the reader understand the point without unnecessary effort?
2. **Is it precise?** Does it describe what Numena actually does or means?
3. **Is it honest?** Does the wording match the strength of the available evidence?
4. **Is it respectful?** Does it treat the reader as a capable professional?
5. **Is it restrained?** Could any words be removed without losing meaningful information?
6. **Is it appropriate?** Does the tone fit the context and sensitivity of the subject?

A piece of writing does not need to satisfy every principle equally in every context. It should, however, avoid contradicting the character of the brand.

---

## Verbal Identity in One Sentence

**Numena speaks with clarity, care, and intellectual restraint. It explains rather than exaggerates, acknowledges complexity without obscuring meaning, and treats its audience as capable of thinking for themselves.**
