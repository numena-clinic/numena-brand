# Logo System

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the principles governing Numena’s logo system, including its variants, usage, and consistency across brand touchpoints.

The goal is to maintain a recognizable, legible, and coherent identity across digital and physical applications.

This document establishes the strategic framework for the logo system. Exact construction measurements, clear-space values, minimum sizes, and technical reproduction specifications should be documented once the artwork has been finalized.

## 2. Role of the Logo

The Numena logo is the primary visual identifier of the brand.

Its purpose is to establish recognition and provide a consistent point of reference across communications, products, and other brand touchpoints.

The logo works alongside Numena’s typography, color palette, imagery, and graphic language. It does not need to communicate the entire brand strategy by itself.

Brand recognition should develop through consistent use of the complete visual identity, not through unnecessary complexity within the logo.

## 3. Design Principles

### 3.1 Recognition over explanation

The logo should be recognizable without requiring an explanation of its meaning.

Its form may express ideas associated with Numena, but it should not attempt to illustrate every aspect of psychology, cognition, or clinical practice.

### 3.2 Simplicity with character

The logo should have a clear visual identity without unnecessary detail.

Its distinctiveness should come from considered form, proportion, typography, or a meaningful relationship between its elements.

### 3.3 Legibility across contexts

The logo should remain identifiable at the sizes and in the environments where it is expected to appear.

If details become indistinguishable at smaller sizes, an alternative variant may be necessary.

### 3.4 Consistency over improvisation

The logo should be treated as an established identity asset, not a graphic element to be redesigned for each application.

Its proportions, internal relationships, and approved color treatments should remain consistent.

### 3.5 Longevity over trends

The logo should be developed for sustained use rather than dependence on short-lived visual trends.

It should remain compatible with Numena’s evolving products and communications without requiring constant redesign.

### Color usage principles

- Use approved color combinations.
- Preserve sufficient contrast against the background.
- Choose the version that remains most legible in the intended context.
- Do not recolor individual logo elements arbitrarily.
- Do not add gradients, outlines, shadows, or other effects unless they belong to an approved variant.

Refer to the Brand Color Strategy for the palette and its intended roles.

## 6. Logo Architecture and Variant Selection

Each logo variant should serve a defined purpose.

| Variant | Primary purpose |
|---|---|
| Primary logo | Default brand identification |
| Secondary logo | Alternative composition where needed |
| Symbol | Compact applications where the symbol remains recognizable |
| Wordmark | Text-based brand identification |
| Light version | Dark backgrounds |
| Dark version | Light backgrounds |
| Monochrome version | Single-color reproduction |

Not every application requires every variant. Maintain only the variants that provide a practical benefit.

Do not assume that a standalone symbol is recognizable simply because it forms part of the primary logo.

## 7. Clear Space and Minimum Size

Clear space protects the logo from nearby text, imagery, borders, and other graphic elements.

## 8. Backgrounds and Placement

### 8.1 Backgrounds

Use approved logo variants on backgrounds that provide sufficient contrast.

When placing the logo over photography or complex imagery, ensure the background does not obscure its shape or reduce legibility.

A simpler background or alternative approved variant may be preferable when contrast is insufficient.

### 8.2 Placement

Logo placement should reflect the purpose and conventions of the medium.

The logo may appear in website headers, footers, title pages, campaign compositions, or other appropriate locations.

It does not need to dominate every composition. Its prominence should reflect the communication context.

### 8.3 Relationship to other elements

The logo should have sufficient visual separation from nearby elements.

Avoid positioning it so closely to headlines, decorative graphics, or other marks that its boundaries become unclear.

When multiple brands appear together, preserve their individual identities and avoid implying an unapproved relationship or endorsement.

## 10. Application Across Touchpoints

The logo system should accommodate Numena’s brand applications without requiring every variant to appear everywhere.

- **Website:** use the primary logo where space and context allow, with a compact variant where justified.
- **Product:** prioritize legibility and recognition within the available space.
- **Social media:** use the standalone symbol only if it remains recognizable at the intended size.
- **Presentations and documents:** maintain consistent placement and scale appropriate to the format.
- **Campaigns:** allow variation in the surrounding composition without altering the logo itself.
- **Partnerships:** preserve the integrity of the Numena logo and maintain clear distinctions between participating brands.
- **Print:** verify that the selected artwork and color treatment reproduce correctly in the intended production process.

These are application principles. Exact dimensions, export formats, and placement specifications should be documented separately where necessary.

## 11. Asset Management

Approved logo artwork should be maintained as a controlled collection of brand assets.

Each approved variant should have a clear filename, intended use, and appropriate file format.

The asset collection should distinguish finalized artwork from concepts, drafts, and deprecated versions.

When the logo changes, update the approved files and related documentation together to prevent inconsistent versions from circulating.

### Asset status

- **Pending:** artwork has not yet been added.
- **In review:** artwork is awaiting approval.
- **Approved:** artwork is finalized and ready for use.
- **Deprecated:** artwork has been replaced and should no longer be used.

## 12. Evaluation Criteria

Before approving a logo variant or application, consider:

1. **Recognition:** Does it remain identifiable as Numena?
2. **Legibility:** Is it clear at the intended display size?
3. **Consistency:** Does it preserve the established identity?
4. **Contrast:** Is it distinguishable from its background?
5. **Purpose:** Does the variant solve a real application need?
6. **Adaptability:** Can it work in the intended medium?
7. **Integrity:** Has the approved artwork been preserved without unauthorized modification?
8. **Necessity:** Does this variant provide a meaningful benefit over existing options?

The objective is a practical, coherent logo system rather than the largest possible collection of variants.
